# Unit 5 Finding: Two Groups That Need Different Responses

**To:** The leadership team
**From:** Nicholas Ku, Trust and Data

Two groups of movies need attention, and they need different responses. Movies 2 and 3 are failing more often than the rest of the catalog. Movies 14 and 15 are not failing at all, because nobody has ever rated them.

For movies 2 and 3, 5 of their 29 ratings were withdrawn as spam, which is 17 percent against a typical 5.7 percent. The five spam ratings came from five different users, spread across the whole of 2023, so this does not look like one account or one burst. Both movies were released in 2023, as were movies 1, 4 and 5. Those five movies hold 7 of the platform's 12 spam withdrawals. They share the Comedy tag, but so do three other movies, so I would not rely on that. I recommend that a reviewer read the five spam ratings and the history of those five accounts within the next two weeks, and report whether the same pattern appears on the other 2023 releases. I would not pause or remove either movie, because nothing in the data points at the movies themselves. The counts are small, so this is a place to start looking and not proof of a problem.

Movies 14 and 15 are a different problem, and a spam review would not help them, because there are no ratings to review. Both are marked available and were added in early 2022, and neither has a genre tag. Every earlier report left them out without saying so, because those reports only counted movies that have ratings. I recommend that the catalog team first confirm whether the two movies appear on the site and in search. If they do not, fixing that comes first. If they do, adding genre tags and watching for ratings over a few weeks would show whether being untagged was the cause. Movie 13 also has no tag and has 11 ratings, so tagging may not be the whole story. If both movies are visible, tagged and still unrated after that, leadership can decide whether to keep paying for them or retire them.

What I can't tell from the data is why movies 14 and 15 have no ratings, whether those five spam accounts are connected in some way the table doesn't show, and whether the 2023 pattern is real or chance. From now on, the movie reports should show all 15 movies, with a zero where there are no ratings.
